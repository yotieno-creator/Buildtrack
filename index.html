Storage and calculation notes

  • Project progress = sum of (task completion % × task weight) ÷ sum of task weights.
  • Remaining budget / budget variance = estimated budget − recorded expenses.
  • Material stock = purchased quantity − used quantity; shortage = max(0, required quantity − stock).
  • localStorage does not synchronize records between browsers or devices. Use JSON export/import to transfer data.
  • This tool is for preliminary monitoring and does not replace certified bills of quantities or professional engineering judgment.
